a letter from your bestie

A little something to keep

There's a letter here for the days when your brain is being a little too mean to you.

Hey, listen to me for a second, okay? ❤️

I know that sometimes it feels like everyone around you is doing better, achieving more, and becoming someone while you're just standing still. I know how easy it is to look at other people and start questioning your own worth. But please, don't be so hard on yourself.

There will always be someone who is better than you at something. Someone richer, smarter, more successful, more attractive, or further ahead in life. But that doesn't mean you're any less valuable as a person. Life isn't a competition, and you're not falling behind just because someone else is moving faster.

You don't have to be the best at everything to be enough. You don't have to prove your worth by achieving something extraordinary. You're allowed to be a work in progress. You're allowed to be confused, to struggle, to make mistakes, and to take your time figuring things out.

And please remember that the way you see yourself when you're feeling down isn't necessarily the way you actually are. You're looking at yourself through the eyes of your insecurities, and they're making you forget all the things that make you who you are.

I wish you could see yourself the way the people who care about you see you. You'd probably understand that you're so much more than what you give yourself credit for.

You don't need to become someone else to deserve happiness, love, or respect. You don't need to be better than everyone around you. You just need to keep becoming a version of yourself that YOU can be proud of, one step at a time.

And even on the days when you don't feel like you're enough, that doesn't make it true. Please don't give up on yourself just because you're not where you want to be yet. ❤️

Always in your corner,
Your Bestie 💗
